## Remaining manual QA and defined limits

- Browser inventory exposes no apps/browsers. Actual pointer dragging, client rollback visuals under simulated failures, select-and-Move browser interaction, stage up/down controls, Sheet focus/keyboard behavior, native confirmation dialogs, mobile overflow/layout and visual QA are **not browser-verified**. Persistence, server authorization and rendered board/form data were tested separately.
- Password-recovery/confirmation email-link redemption remains manual through the local inbox. Live tests verify requests, authenticated password update/login/logout, not browser redemption.
- Kanban shows up to20 cards per matching stage, with exact full column counts and a link to the paginated table. Table, notes, timeline and follow-ups paginate20. Duplicate suggestions cap at10; customer search shows the first20 matching authorized contacts.
- Duplicate suggestions are exact normalized-phone/email matches for leads; customers also support full-name matches. No fuzzy scoring or automatic merge exists. Hidden customer records are not disclosed by conversion matching.
- Sales viewers can read internal sales notes in their authorized branch; there is no separate lead-note permission in this phase. Eligibility requires active CRM-linked sales employees; ordinary/offline HR records are not automatically sales staff.
- Leads have one branch; shared customers retain their all-assigned-branches authorization rule. Existing-customer conversion requires the lead branch already be associated with that customer. It never expands customer access or replaces contact fields implicitly.
- Converted lead contact data is frozen; conversion undo is not implemented. Notes and follow-up completion retain sales traceability. Lost/disqualified records require authorized reopening before conversion. Archives preserve all history.
- Follow-up times use the business timezone then UTC; invalid dates/DST gaps are rejected, and ambiguous DST times use the resolved valid offset. No automated reminders or real lesson records are created.
- Metrics use the non-archived lead creation cohort with inclusive business-local date boundaries and current outcomes. Conversion date is not used as a different denominator. Overdue counts are for pending tasks attached to that same lead cohort.
- No payments, external messaging, student/course enrollment, lessons, academic progress, parent portal, payroll calculation or production deployment is implemented. Historical dependency audit findings were not re-audited; no dependencies changed.
